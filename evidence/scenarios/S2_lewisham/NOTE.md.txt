Scenario 2, Lewisham Council. Note on captures 2 and 2b.

These two frames show the same record, H. Ademola, seven days apart.

On 25 August the deadline of 26 August was one day away. The record sat in
the Due within 7 days band, open and answerable.

On 1 September the record is seven days past its deadline, still open, and
counted in Open and overdue.

No action was taken on the record between the two captures. The gap was not
planned. It arose because work on the project paused for several days.

The system surfaced the deadline correctly at both points. The request still
went late.

This is the Lewisham failure occurring inside the artefact rather than a
staged demonstration of it. Lewisham's 35% was made of individual requests
that were visible, answerable, and not answered. It also gives concrete form
to the limitation identified in the Mid-Point Review replaying documented
scenarios cannot establish whether staff would act on what the system
surfaces. Here they did not, and the outcome was the one the ICO reprimanded.
